Terms & Methodology

This calculator computes income tax for salaried individuals under both the Old and New Tax Regime. All figures are in Indian Rupees and follow the Income Tax Act, 1961 as applicable for AY 2026–27.

Inputs

Income Fields

Field How it's used
Gross Salary Total salary before any deductions, the figure on your Form 16 or salary slip.
Income from Interest Interest from savings bank accounts and other taxable interests from banks.
Rental Income Amount of annual rental income received on let-out properties.
Other Income Freelance receipts, gifts exceeding ₹50,000, or any other taxable income.
Professional Tax State-level tax deducted by your employer (typically up to ₹2,400/yr).
Computed Gross Total Income = Salary + Taxable Interest + Rental Income + Other Income

Scope

Who This Calculator Applies To

This calculator is designed for salaried resident individuals below 60 years of age. It does not apply to senior citizens (60+), NRIs, or self-employed individuals, as different tax rules and exemption limits apply to those categories. Capital gains income, marginal surcharge relief, and TDS already paid are also not accounted for. Based on Union Budget 2025 provisions for FY 2025–26 (AY 2026–27).

⚠ Disclaimer

This calculator is for informational and planning purposes only and is not a substitute for professional tax advice. Tax laws are subject to amendment without notice. The payable figure does not account for TDS already deducted, any excess TDS is eligible for a refund when you file your ITR.

For filing returns or detailed tax planning, refer to the Income Tax Department's official portal at incometax.gov.in or consult a qualified Chartered Accountant.
